# Open Source and Commercial Model

InnerOS Agentic Platform is licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

## What the license allows

The open-source code may be used, modified, redistributed, embedded in other
systems, and used commercially, subject to the Apache License 2.0 terms.

## What InnerChispa can still sell

Open source does not prevent InnerChispa from selling products and services
around InnerOS. Commercial offerings may include:

- installation and deployment;
- managed hosting;
- enterprise support and SLAs;
- architecture and integration services;
- private connectors and customer-specific modules;
- security hardening and compliance work;
- observability, monitoring, backup, and recovery operations;
- hardware/appliance bundles;
- training and implementation services;
- commercial vertical solutions built on top of InnerOS;
- private operational configuration, customer data, and deployment tooling.

The public repository is the reusable platform core. Customer-specific
credentials, infrastructure topology, operational data, and private business
modules are intentionally kept outside the public repository.

## Trademark boundary

The Apache License 2.0 grants broad rights to the software but does not grant
a general trademark license. Forks and derivative products should not imply
endorsement by InnerChispa or present themselves as an official InnerOS
distribution unless authorized.

## Contribution model

Contributions intentionally submitted to this repository are accepted under
the Apache License 2.0 unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Why Apache-2.0

Apache-2.0 was selected because it is broadly recognized, business-friendly,
compatible with commercial services and products, and includes an explicit
patent grant and patent-termination provision.

This document is an operational explanation of the project model, not a
replacement for the LICENSE text.
