# Privacy Policy for InnerSpark LinkedIn Automation App

**Last Updated:** June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our internal automation application ("the App") handles data. The App is developed and operated by InnerChispa LLC for the sole purpose of automating content distribution on LinkedIn.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
* **OAuth Tokens:** The App requests and securely stores OAuth access tokens provided by LinkedIn to authorize and execute automated posts on behalf of the administrator.
* **Content Data:** The App processes text, links, and media explicitly provided by our internal servers to publish them to LinkedIn.
* **No Personal Data Collection:** The App does not collect, harvest, scrape, or store personal data, emails, or profiles of third-party LinkedIn users.

### 2. Data Storage and Security
* All authentication tokens and publication logs are stored securely within our private on-premise infrastructure.
* No data processed by this application is ever shared, rented, or sold to third-party entities.
